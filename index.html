The Vision

New York's Cannabis Community on the Course

A day for New York's cannabis insiders to convene, connect, and celebrate the season.

The Croptober Cup is a gathering for the people who make New York's cannabis industry run — the budtenders, buyers, growers, and brand teams who work the counters, stock the shelves, and cultivate the product day in and day out.

This is a day to set down the work and enjoy a round of golf, a game of cornhole, a meal from The Grove, and a chance to meet some of our favorite growers from around the Hudson Valley. We're inviting select growers to exhibit and showcase flower from this year's harvest.

Hudson Valley

The Format Adapts to You

Small field? Double-elimination keeps everyone in the hunt. Big turnout? We split into pools, then run a knockout to a champion. How ever it breaks, it's best-of-3 to 21 at regulation 27ft.

The Rules

  • Distance: Regulation 27 feet between boards
  • Scoring: 3 pts bag in hole, 1 pt bag on board

Weather & What to Wear

Late October in the Hudson Valley is cool and changeable. Expect a high near 55°F and a morning low in the mid-40s. It can feel considerably colder on the course before the sun is up and while you're moving between holes, so plan for a cool start.

Dress in layers you can shed as it warms. A base layer, a sweater or fleece, and a windproof outer layer work well. Bring a hat and gloves for the morning nine — the first few holes are the coldest. Comfortable footwear matters more than style; the course can be damp.

Plan for the chance of rain — October averages about 4 inches in this area, so it's a real possibility. Bring rain gear and an umbrella. The event runs rain or shine, and there's covered seating under the tent for lunch and the cornhole tournament.
